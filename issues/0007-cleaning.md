## Question

How does the visitor wipe patina away?

## Resolution

Two separate controls:

- **Reset** — clears both dirt and wear on every component. Factory new.
- **Cloth** — a mode where the visitor scrubs components individually by hand. Removes **dirt only**, locally where wiped; **wear remains**.

Cloth feel is still in the map's fog.
